03 / THE METHOD

Taste makes the loop meaningful.

  1. 01Choose a real problem

    Who benefits? What changes for them, and how would we know?

  2. 02Challenge the objective

    Would chasing this metric violate truth, dignity, consent or access?

  3. 03Make a falsifiable forecast

    Compare alternatives, expected learning, assumptions and cost.

  4. 04Run the smallest honest test

    Measure real outcomes, including failures, abstentions and disagreements.

  5. 05Improve our judgment

    Calibrate forecasts and learn which problems truly deserved our effort.

✳

We keep **software correctness**, **research judgement** and **real-world impact** on separate scorecards. Passing a simulation isn't helping a person. The work continues until reality tells us more.
